Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Odd Pear Studio, LLC ("Odd Pear Studio," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information in connection with our website (oddpearstudio.com) and our mobile application, Twyne (together, the "Service").

Our Service is offered only to residents of the United States who are at least 18 years old. If you do not reside in the United States, please do not use the Service. This Policy is intended to comply with United States federal and state law and does not address the laws of any other jurisdiction.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide to us

  • Account and profile information: name, date of birth, gender, sexual orientation and dating preferences, photos, bio/prompts, and other information you choose to add to your profile.
  • Contact information: email address and, if you provide it, phone number.
  • Communications: messages you exchange with other members through Twyne, and communications you send to our support team.
  • Payment information: if you purchase a subscription, payment is processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play, using RevenueCat as our subscription management provider on Android. We do not receive or store your full payment card number.

Information collected automatically

  • Location: with your permission, we collect your device's precise geolocation to power matching and distance features.
  • Device and usage information: device type, operating system, app version, and how you interact with the Service, collected through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Push notification tokens: collected through Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can deliver notifications to your device.

Information from other sources

We may receive limited account status information from Apple, Google, and RevenueCat (for example, whether a subscription is active) to manage your account.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and manage your account and profile;
  • Operate matching, messaging, and other core features of Twyne;
  • Process subscription payments and manage billing status;
  • Send you transactional emails (e.g., email verification, password reset) through our email provider, Resend;
  • With your consent or as permitted by law, send you marketing communications about Odd Pear Studio or Twyne — you can opt out at any time using the unsubscribe link in any such email;
  • Maintain the safety and integrity of the Service, including detecting fraud, spam, and violations of our Terms of Service;
  • Respond to your requests and provide customer support;
  • Comply with our legal obligations.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We share information with:

  • Other members: information you add to your profile (e.g., photos, bio, prompts) is visible to other members as part of the Service.
  • Service providers: companies that help us operate the Service, including Supabase (database and backend hosting), Firebase/Google (analytics, crash reporting, push notifications), RevenueCat (subscription management), Resend (email delivery), and Apple and Google (app distribution and payment processing). These providers may only use your information to provide services to us.
  • Legal and safety reasons: if required by law, legal process, or government request, or if we believe it is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Odd Pear Studio, our members, or the public.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to standard confidentiality protections.
  • With your consent: in any other circumstances where you direct us to share your information.
